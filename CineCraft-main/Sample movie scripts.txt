Gritty Rayalaseema Action (Similar to Pushpa / Rangasthalam)
Story Line: In a sun-scorched village in Kadapa, a young potter's son 
unknowingly unearths an ancient idol on a ruthless landlord's illegal mining site. 
This act sparks a violent feud, forcing him to rise from a simple artisan 
to a reluctant leader of a rebellion to protect his clan's heritage.
Genre: Action Drama
Tone: Gritty, Raw, Rustic, High-Tension
Setting: A dusty, red-soil village in Kadapa district, 1990s. Limestone quarries, Tata Sumos, and traditional stone houses.
Character Name: Shiva
Character Traits: Stoic, rugged, muddy hands, intense gaze, loyal to family, reluctant warrior.


Godavari Family Rom-Com (Similar to Seethamma Vakitlo...)
Story Line: During the chaotic preparations for a massive joint-family wedding
in a lush green village on the banks of the Godavari, 
a city-bred software engineer falls for the bride’s headstrong cousin. 
They must navigate quirky relatives, misunderstood traditions, and a missing wedding ring.
Genre: Romantic Comedy
Tone: Vibrant, Festive, Humorous, Feel-good
Setting: Konaseema during monsoon. Coconut groves, houseboats on the river, and a large traditional ancestral home (Manduva Logili).
Character Name: Karthik
Character Traits: Charming but anxious, urban, fish-out-of-water, quick-witted, respects culture but doesn't understand it.
